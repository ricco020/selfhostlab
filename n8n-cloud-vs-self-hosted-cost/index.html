How the calculator decides

  • Cloud plan: the smallest plan whose monthly executions cover yours. Starter: 2,500 executions, 20 EUR/mo billed annually. Pro: 10,000 executions, 50 EUR/mo billed annually. Above that, the pricing page lists Business (40,000 executions, 667 EUR/mo) as self-hosted only, so cloud goes to Enterprise.
  • Server size: executions do not decide it, memory does. In our test n8n ran 26.5 executions per second with 10 in parallel (millions per month) and peaked at 780 MB of RAM. With PostgreSQL and a proxy we keep 4 GB. With workers or other apps, 8 GB.
  • Cost over time: promo price for the first 24 months, then the renewal price Hostinger shows on the plan card. That is the line most comparisons leave out.

What the hosting bill does not show

Self-hosting moves work to you: n8n updates, OS patches, backups of the database and the encryption key, HTTPS. Cloud includes them. Some features also differ between editions (the pricing page compares Cloud and self-hosted plan by plan). If you would not notice a broken backup for a month, Cloud Starter is the safer buy.

FAQ

Is self-hosted n8n cheaper than n8n Cloud?
In hosting cost, usually yes: $6.49/mo for a 4 GB VPS on a 24-month term ($11.99 at renewal) against 20 EUR/mo for Starter and 50 EUR/mo for Pro, billed annually. The price is your maintenance time.
How many executions can a small VPS handle?
Far more than the cloud plans include: 13.5 per second one at a time in our test. Memory decides the server size, not executions.
What happens above 10,000 executions per month on n8n Cloud?
Pro stops at 10,000. Business is listed as self-hosted only, so larger cloud volumes go to Enterprise, priced on request.
